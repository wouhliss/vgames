A2-T12: controller mapping and emulation decision. PlayStation and Switch Pro pads map to an Xbox 360 layout by position (optionally by Nintendo label), with radial stick deadzones, anti-deadzone, inverted Y, trigger scaling and validated per-package remap profiles. The 07 §3 per-launch decision covers compat layers, the never/always override and backend availability. The SDL input thread and the virtual-pad backends are still to come.
